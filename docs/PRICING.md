# Baraza Protocol: Pricing & Business Model One-Pager

## 1. Overview & Vision

Baraza Protocol bridges grassroots African collective finance—chamas, SACCOs, mutual aid groups, cooperatives, and community DAOs—with resilient on-chain governance and multi-chain settlement rails (Stellar, Solana, EVM). 

Our economic model is built on three core pillars:
1. **Zero Governance Friction**: Members **never pay gas or transaction fees to vote or submit informal proposals**.
2. **Accessible Low-Barrier Onboarding**: A nominal, one-off activation cost with cohort grant waiver mechanics.
3. **Enterprise Compliance Monetisation**: Sustainable protocol revenues generated from statutory compliance exports, verified legal wrapper tools, and premium AI advisory for formal entities (SACCOs, registered cooperatives, NGOs).

---

## 2. Membership Activation & Fee Structure

### A. One-Off Account Activation
* **Fee**: **KES 20 (~$0.15 USD)** per unique member identity.
* **Settlement Rail**: Settled via M-Pesa direct onramp into Kotani Pay / Stellar G-account reserve.
* **Purpose**: Subsidises protocol gas abstraction, phone/identity verification, and anti-sybil state creation.

### B. Activation Waiver Pool (Grant-Funded Sponsorship)
To preserve universal access for low-income rural chamas and informal self-help groups:
* **Cohort Sponsorship Pool**: Institutional grant funds (BAD DAO treasury, Stellar Community Fund, Solana Foundation, Celo GoodDollar grants) fund automated waiver pools.
* **Mechanism**: Community admins can apply for or draw from waiver quotas, activating member batches at zero net cost to the individual participant.

### C. The Mtaa Cost-Crossover Point
Addressing the tension between the nominal KES 20 activation fee and the protocol promise that *"members never pay to vote"*:
* **Crossover Threshold**: After the 3rd governance interaction (vote cast, proposal sponsored, or dues streak milestone logged), the protocol gas-subsidy reserve amortises the onboarding cost to < KES 0.05 per interaction.
* **Net Lifetime Result**: Collective governance remains 100% free at point of interaction across the entire lifecycle of the chama.

## 3. Product & Subscription Tiers

Baraza implements a unified DAO kernel across all tiers; legal wrapper tiers add metadata verification, regulatory exports, and specialised AI statutory reasoning without bifurcating the underlying blockchain contracts.

| Tier | Target Persona | Features & Capabilities | Pricing Model |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Informal (Community)** | Informal chamas, study circles, social clubs, family savings pools | Full DAO kernel (on-chain voting, proposals, shared Stellar treasury, BRZA minting/allocations, retro rounds, badges, dues streaks, baseline Akili Council AI) | **Free** ($0 / month) |
| **Self-Declared** | Self-identified SACCOs, unregistered cooperatives, pre-incorporation investment clubs | Everything in Informal + "Self-Declared" community badge + standard financial ledger exports (CSV/JSON) + multi-admin permissioning | **KES 1,500 / month (~$12 USD)** |
| **Verified Business** | Registered SACCOs (SASRA), Cooperatives (Cooperative Societies Act), registered NGOs, LLPs | Everything in Self-Declared + "Verified Business" trust badge + statutory Akili Council mode (Kofi cites statutory sections; Zara audits reserve ratios) + officer-tied multisig policies + **SASRA-format reports*** | **KES 6,500 / month (~$50 USD)** or **KES 65,000 / year** (annual discount) |

> *\*Regulatory Disclaimer: "SASRA-format reports" generate structured financial extracts matching regulatory reporting layouts for SACCO supervisory authorities; formal certification is pending ongoing institutional review.*
